Purpose:
BluSpellbook helps Blue Mages keep track of spells in HorizonXI. It shows which spells you know, where to learn missing spells, which spells make job traits, and which useful moves nearby enemies have used.

The addon only reads information from your game. It does not change anything on the server or send extra packets.

How to Install:

1. Put the `bluspellbook` folder inside Ashita's `addons` folder.
2. Start FFXI and type `/addon load bluspellbook` in the chat box.

The main window only appears when Blue Mage is your main job or support job.

Commands

- `/bsb` — Open or close BluSpellbook.
- `/bsb show` — Open BluSpellbook.
- `/bsb hide` — Close BluSpellbook.
- `/bsb refresh` — Check your learned spells again.
- `/bsb test` — Show test messages so you can resize and move them.
- `/bsb help` — Show the command list in the chat box.

You can also use `/bluspellbook` or `/bluspells` instead of `/bsb`.

What the Addon Does

- Lists Blue Magic spells from level 1 through 75.
- Shows learned spells in green.
- Shows spells you are ready to learn in yellow.
- Shows spells you are not ready to learn in red.
- Lets you view Required, Recommended, or All spells.
- Shows a spell's description, needed Blue Magic skill, and known places to learn it.
- Shows Blue Mage job traits and the best spells to use for each trait.
- Checks your learned spells when the addon loads, while you play, and after you learn a spell. You can also press **Refresh**.
- Shows nearby enemies that can teach useful Blue Magic. It also counts how many times they use a move you can learn.

Options

- Change the size of the main window and screen messages.
- Turn the learned-spell and enemy-move messages on or off.
- Show or hide the last three characters of an enemy's ID.
- Keep detecting enemy moves even when you already know the spell.
- Change how dark the enemy-list background is.
- Hide the window during combat or while supported FFXI menus are open.
- Press **Test Messages** to preview and move the screen messages.
- Press **Save Settings** to keep your changes after reloading the addon.
- Press **Reset All Settings** to return everything to its original settings.
- Press **README** to open this guide again.

Important Things to Know

- To move the enemy-move list during normal play, hold **Shift** and drag it with the mouse.
- Level sync lowers your usable Blue Magic skill. This can make a spell appear red even when you could normally learn it.
- Do not block game messages that say an enemy readies or uses an ability. BluSpellbook sometimes needs these messages to recognize enemy moves correctly.
- If you use XIUI, open its Target Bar settings and turn on **Show Enemy ID** and **Convert to Hex**. This makes its enemy IDs match BluSpellbook.
- Some learning locations may be missing or unconfirmed. To report a missing location or addon problem, contact Tavarian on the HorizonXI Discord.
